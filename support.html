Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an account?
No. The app works entirely on your device — no sign-up, no account, no login.

Is my scan history stored anywhere online?
No. Everything — your scan and generation history, and any saved images — stays on your device only.

Why does the app need camera access?
Camera access is used only to scan QR codes. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Why does the app need photo library access?
Only to save a QR code you've generated, and only when you tap "Save to Photos."
