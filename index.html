Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the QR Code Scanner & Generator app ("the App") handles information. We built this App to work entirely on your device, with no account, sign-up, or server involved.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Everything the App does — scanning, generating, and saving QR codes — happens locally on your device.

Camera Access

The App requests camera access to scan QR codes. Camera data is processed entirely on your device to detect and decode QR codes. No camera footage or images are recorded, stored, or sent anywhere by the App.

Photo Library Access

When you choose to save a generated QR code, the App requests permission to add images to your photo library. This only happens when you explicitly tap "Save to Photos" — the App does not access, browse, or read your existing photo library, and only ever adds the image you chose to save.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps a local history of QR codes you've scanned or generated, stored only on your device using local storage. This history is never transmitted off your device, and is deleted if you clear it from within the App or uninstall the App.

  • Scanned or generated QR code content
  • The type of content (e.g. link, Wi-Fi, contact, text)
  • A timestamp of when it was scanned or generated
  • A display name you optionally set in the App's Settings, stored only on your device

Third Parties

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and does not share any information with third parties, because no information leaves your device in the first place.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
